# Results 

## Design Challenge 1: Artists and Note takers can both use to full potential
People really enjoy the macros on physical buttons. They need the buttons to be right above their grip. There also needs to be some type of simple status indicator of what the buttons are doing / updating. 
## Design Challenge 2: The Pen has a very high quality feel
People want the pen to be very secure and durable. They want the buttons to have tactile response. People refuse to have their smart pen be more than 1 part. 
## Design Challenge 3: Pen Has Different Modes for Different Users
Definetly have at least one colored status LED. People in general liked the ability to have a back functionality. Don't make it too complicated.
